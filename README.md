# Invoice Builder

A free, simple invoice maker for contractors and subcontractors.

**Use it here:** https://rizzytechcfo-coder.github.io/invoice/

## How to use
1. Fill in your company info, the customer (Bill To), and the project.
2. Add your materials, parts and labor lines. Totals calculate automatically.
3. Click **Create PDF**. A clean, printable invoice downloads to your device.

Nothing you type is uploaded or stored anywhere; everything runs in your browser.
